Flat-shaded isometric objects with clean dark ink outlines on a plain light background. A carefully inked technical
illustration crossed with Minecraft's own map shading: every surface is one flat colour, and form comes from the four
shades of one hue, never from gradients.
- Faces: lit top face = lightest shade, left face = second shade, right face = third shade, cast shadow or deep
  recess = darkest shade.
- Outlines: solid ink, even, fairly bold weight. No sketchy hatching, no pencil grain.
- Background: plain paper, the lightest neutral colour. Nothing else behind the subject.
- Fills: large flat areas only. No gradients, glows, soft shadows, watercolour washes, textures, noise or photo detail.
- Surfaces: perfectly clean flat colour. No wood grain, scratches, rust spots, mottling or speckle.
- Framing: the whole subject fits inside the image with a wide margin of plain paper on every side, centred. No
  frame, border, bars, vignette or cut-off objects.
- Words: none. No letters, numbers, labels, signs, logos or UI anywhere.
- Few objects, drawn large.
